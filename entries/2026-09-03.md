# you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love
review_score 8
you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love is an album by Olivia Rodrigo. I was listening to the radio, and they were interviewing her about her new single and music festival called Daisy Chain Fields. It seems like a positive project, but I didn't look too closely. 
Either way, this album was nice, but not for me. Subject isn't something that I generally connect with. The pop vibe and beat was pretty nice, but I didn't really feel like the album has anything particularly for me. 
Overall, I gave it an 8 out of 10 because it seems like a reasonable album that I would recommend to a friend if they were considering it, but I wouldn't actively recommend it. None of the songs were added to my playlist, but my way was my favorite song because of its beat.
