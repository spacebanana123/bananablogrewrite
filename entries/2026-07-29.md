# I Love My Computer
review_score 10
*I Love My Computer* by Ninajirachi is an album that I really like. Lately I've been wondering if some of the charm of music has worn off for me and I've some how become jaded against music. Having listened to this album, I'm more confident that I do really have music that I like. I really think this is album that will stay with me for a while. 
Some context I think will be helpful. Lately I've been upset with much of the way that the internet broadly, but also specific technologies like AI are turning out. I think part of this is a response to the uncertainty that these changes bring. I'm a programmer, so I think its really only natural that I find myself unsettled by unsettling changes to the technical sphere. On the whole, I don't like the way things are moving and it really frustrates me.
I think the way that Ninajirachi's song really hits me is in a sense of optimism about computers and technology. Personally I don't have that kind of belief in computing, at least for a long time, but "Sing Good" really hit a sense of optimism. I remember the smaller coding projects that I did when I was younger that truly, in the grand scheme of things, weren't super impressive, but felt really cool to me. I remember following <a href="https://beej.us/guide/bgnet/html/">Beej's Guide</a> when I was younger and coding tictactoe using sockets. That was so cool, I could not believe what I managed to do with it. I still have a <a href="https://youtu.be/cdQeRU-Ljuw">video</a> of it, but I have tragically lost the source code. In some way, this album has managed to restart that sense of possibility. 
Stylistically, this album feels very close to <a href="https://spacebanana.dpdns.org/music#pullup_to_busan_4_morE_hypEr_summEr_it%E2%80%99s_gonna_bE_a_fuckin_moviE">pullup to busan</a>. Much of what I said would apply here. I do think the album stands on its own, even if it isn't deep in nostalgia for me. It's catchy, impressive, and mixes a lot of cool styles. I enjoyed it a lot. I really like the message of "Sing Good" and "Delete" is also a favorite of mine. Both songs made it in my main playlist. Overall, a 10 out of 10.
