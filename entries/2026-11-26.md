# HALO
review_score 9.0
HALO is an album by Tiffany Day. This is my first album review after a very long time and I hope I can be more consistent about this in the future. I firmly think that I really like Hyperpop as a genre. If you knew me in person, I'm not sure if you would come to that conclusion because I tend to not like really loud music. For example, I don't really like Nevermind by Nirvana. It is genuinely a little too loud and abrasive, but Hyperpop doesn't feel that way for me. 
I do wonder what is so different about hyperpop that I don't dislike its loudness as much, but I honestly can't say much besides the fact that I perceive it  as less loud. It could have something to do with having less bass, but I lack confidence in saying that. 
Any ways, I really quite like HALO. While procrastinating writing this review I ended up listening to this album like 4-5 times which is a fair bit more than I usually with listen to an album. The writing is tight, I enjoy the melodies, and I could feel myself just vibing to the music. 
Overall a 9.0 out of 10, but it could be that I'm rating a little higher because I'm coming back to this after a while. My favorite song was AMERICAN GIRL. I added it to my main playlist.
