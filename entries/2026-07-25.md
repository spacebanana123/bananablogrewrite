# Weezer
review_score 7
Weezer by Weezer is actually not called the Blue Album, which I was surprised by. To be honest, this review will be shorter mostly because i didn't really end up feeling the Weezer style. I know some people who really like Weezer, but I mostly think that my substantive preferences are against this type of album. 
Overall, I walked away from the album thinking that I hadn't really lost anything, but that I would probably never listen to one again. 7 out of 10.
