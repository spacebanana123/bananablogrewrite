# Bang Bang
review_score 6
Bang Bang by Dispatch is a bit of a strange case. I intuitively feel like I should have liked this album, but I don't. I got recommended this album by a friend and its sounds are pretty similar to some songs that I've liked the in past. I think part of the initial appeal is that the lyrics are interesting. They have meaning that seems to reflect some deeper considerations about life. I like that kind of songs that I can relate to. 
Yet, I think the quality of the lyrics in someways ends up detracting from the songs. When I am listening to songs, I don't pause or review the lyrics beyond what I can hear directly from the music. When the lyrics are a bit confusing and require more thought, I don't want to pause the songs or search up the lyrics to understand it. It is because of the uncertainty in exact meaning that I find listening can be frustrating. 
Overall, I think there are also some stylistic issues for me. The songs tend to be a little slower paced and have a limited range of tones. I rated the album 6 out of 10, which might be a little harsh, but I do feel like in some ways these songs would have been better as poems.
